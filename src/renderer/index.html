    A FEW WORDS GO A LONG WAY

    Make the web your own.

    Use your voice, your keyboard, or the controls on the page.

    Start here

    1. Connect your Google and TypeSafe services.
    2. Open a website, or explore the practice page.
    3. Hold Control + Option + Space while speaking, or hold the Hold to speak button. Speak after the tone. Release to send.
    4. Each page opens in Page Reader. Use ↑ and ↓ to choose a title, Enter to open it, and R to read it. Turn off “Read titles as I move” to read only on request.

    Press Esc to stop in Svara. From the controlled browser, use ⌃ ⌥ Esc.
